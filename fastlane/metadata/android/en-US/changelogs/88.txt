- Based on RustDesk 1.5.0, with its stability and audio fixes.
- Requires Android 7.0 or later.
- Credentials encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore; optional local password for the settings.
- Watermark with technician, time and session code when the remote PC requires it.
- Privacy mode offered when connecting to a PC; can be turned off.
- Network forwarding and Webcam forwarding menus: ports in both directions and the phone camera as a webcam on the remote PC.
