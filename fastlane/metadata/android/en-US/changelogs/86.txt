- Based on RustDesk 1.5.0, with its stability and audio fixes.
- Requires Android 7.0 or later.
- Saved passwords and credentials are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore.
- Optional local password to protect the settings.
- When the remote PC requires it, the session shows a watermark with technician, time and session code.
- Asks whether to enable privacy mode when connecting to a PC; it can be turned off.
